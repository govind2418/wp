Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Leela Infra Solution collects and uses information through its WhatsApp Business messaging (via the WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta).

Information we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and display name, when you message us or when we send you a message.
  • The content of messages exchanged with our business number, for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

How we use this information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To send order updates, appointment reminders, or other business notifications you have opted in to receive.
  • We do not sell or share your information with third parties, except with Meta/WhatsApp as required to deliver messages.

Data retention

Message history is retained only as long as needed to provide support and is not used for any purpose beyond business communication with you.

Your choices

You can stop receiving messages from us at any time by replying "STOP" or by blocking our business number in WhatsApp.
